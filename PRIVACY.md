# Privacy Policy

Scholar Agent Feed is a personal research automation operated by Jun-Yu Pan.

## Data accessed

The application accesses the operator's Gmail account only to read Google Scholar alert emails required to identify newly published academic papers.

## Use of data

Email data is processed solely to extract public academic-paper metadata, including titles, authors, abstracts, identifiers, and publication links.

The application does not use Gmail data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## Data storage

Email credentials, OAuth tokens, and private email contents are not published in the public feed.

The public repository stores only public paper metadata and automatically generated research summaries. Email contents are processed within a private GitHub Actions workflow.

## Data sharing

Gmail data is not sold or shared with third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Temporary workflow data may be discarded after processing. The operator may stop access at any time by revoking the application's Google Account authorization and deleting the corresponding GitHub Actions secrets.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the email address listed as the application's user-support email.
